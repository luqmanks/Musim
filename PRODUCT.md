# Musim Field Shift

Musim Field Shift helps Malaysian farmers compare crop-rotation options using field location, farm constraints, personal priorities, NASA-derived climate context, and local judgment.

## Audience

The primary user is a farmer or farm adviser who may have limited geospatial experience, intermittent connectivity, and little time for technical interpretation.

## Core job

The product guides the user through four tasks: identify a field, describe current farm conditions, rank practical goals, and compare rotation strategies. A point estimate remains available when drawing a field boundary is impractical.

## Product commitments

- Lead with practical field meaning and explain scientific terms where they appear.
- Show uncertainty, data timing, and the limits of satellite observations.
- Treat recommendations as conversation support, not prescriptions.
- Preserve farmer and agronomist judgment, especially for drainage, equipment, seed access, markets, and crop timing.
- Keep essential controls usable on small screens and in outdoor conditions.

## Success

A farmer can reach a comprehensible comparison, understand why each option scored as it did, and know what to validate locally before changing a rotation.
